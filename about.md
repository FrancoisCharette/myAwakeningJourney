# About this journal

A few years ago I started a YouTube channel,
[François Ventures Into Awakening]({{ site.youtube_channel }}), to share
observations from my spiritual journey. I recorded a handful of videos and then
stopped — not because the journey ended, but because video is the wrong medium
for it. Recording and editing is slow, and worse, a published video is frozen:
when my understanding of a subject evolved, the video couldn't evolve with it.

This site is the format the journey actually needed. Every post is a living
document. When my thinking changes — sometimes because of a comment someone
left here — I revise the post and note how it evolved. The revision history is
part of the point.

## What I write about

- **Spirituality** — practices, experiences, and questions from the path.
- **Human nature** — behaviors I notice in myself and others, and what they reveal.
- **Technology & human development** — how accelerating technology shapes (and distorts) how we grow.

## About me

I'm François Charette. By profession I'm an engineer —
[that side of me lives here]({{ site.professional_site }}). This site is the
other side.

## Join the conversation

Every post has a comment section. I read everything, respond when I have
something worth saying, and revise posts when someone shows me a better angle.
Disagreement offered in good faith is a gift here.
