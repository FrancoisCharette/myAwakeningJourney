---
title: "Why this site exists (and why the videos stopped)"
categories: [Spirituality]
---

A few years ago I started
[a YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@FrancoisVenturesIntoAwakening)
to share observations from my spiritual journey. After a few videos, I stopped.

Partly it was practical: recording and editing video is time-consuming, and it
was never my medium. But the deeper problem was this — **a published video is
frozen, and a journey is not.** When my thinking on a subject evolved, the video
stayed exactly where I had left it, confidently saying things I no longer fully
agreed with. Re-editing and republishing every time my understanding shifted was
never going to work.

So this site works the other way around. Every post here is a living document:

- When my understanding evolves, I revise the post.
- Each revision gets noted, so you can see *how* the thinking moved, not just
  where it landed.
- The comment section under every post is not decoration — it's the mechanism.
  If you see something I'm missing, say so. Some of the best course corrections
  come from a view I couldn't have reached alone.

The topics will be the same ones the channel was meant for: spirituality,
the patterns I notice in human nature, and what accelerating technology is
doing to human development.

Welcome to the journey. It's under revision — permanently, and on purpose.
