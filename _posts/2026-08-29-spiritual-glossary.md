The reason I feel compelled to make this glossary is that English is not my first language, and since the start of my awakening I have realized that all of my life I have been using the English language incorrectly, or at least imprecisely. What I mean by that is that as long as the message seemed to be understood by people, I simply moved on. Unless it was a friend or a close co-worker, most of the time people would not necessarily understand me the first time we met and talked because of my accent and my wording, so I often had to repeat myself using different terms just to convey the message.

In the last few years, I realized that such an approach to using the English language is OK for transactional exchanges, but it is not precise enough to discuss or share ideas efficiently. It can easily generate unnecessary confusion of meaning, which is why I decided to create this glossary post. I will try to update it regularly as needed, and use it in all of my posts to be "more precise" in my messaging.

As a side note, my awakening allowed me to notice that language "imprecision" or "misuse" is one of the approaches or methods used for propaganda messaging.

> A simple example of propaganda that comes to mind is that all of my life I have heard it mentioned in the news, documentaries, etc. how governments "waste" money to buy or build this or that. Nowadays, with social media, it is clear it was never "waste" but actual "fraud". The difference between "waste" and "fraud" is drastic. "Waste" represents incompetence, mismanagement, errors, etc., so there is nothing you can really do about it, while "fraud" is a VOLUNTARY criminal act to enrich someone. That kind of language misuse is, for me, intentional and not an "error"; it _represents propaganda_ to control the emotional response of the public. They explicitly used the term "waste" to elicit disappointment, frustration, etc., nothing more. The use of the term "fraud" would have elicited anger, demands for action, etc.

### Awakening
As Eckhart Tolle teaches it, awakening is **a shift in consciousness, not an achievement of the mind**. Nothing is added to you and nothing is learned. What happens is that awareness separates from thought — you stop being the stream of thinking and become the space in which thinking is noticed. 

Awakening does not destroy the Ego. Form still has function; awakened consciousness acts through a person, a personality, a life. What ends is the Ego’s claim to be the one in charge.[^claude] As an often-quoted saying, usually attributed to Zen, puts it:

> Be a master of the mind, NOT mastered by the mind!


> __Personal Side Note:__
> My Ego has always interpreted, or conceptualized, awakening as some kind of state. What I recently realized and observed in various YouTube videos is that awakening is actually a never-ending journey or process that has no end-point or destination. Awakening is designed to be part of, and used in, this Space-Time reality in conjunction with the Ego.
> 
> This observation explains why my Ego constantly tries to judge whether someone is awakened or not. That judgement was always based on evaluating a state. Now, I know that was an "error", and that someone awake is simply on a "journey" at a different phase or level, like me!


> __About Eckhart Tolle:__
> Tolle’s own turning point was the sentence that ran through him at 29[^oprah], in the depths of a suicidal depression: “I cannot live with myself any longer.”[^tolle-now] He then noticed the strangeness of it — if that is true, there must be two of him: the “I” and the “self” he cannot live with.[^onbeing] One of them, he concluded, must not be real. That split is the whole teaching in miniature.



### Default Mode Network, DMN
Neuroscience identified the brain's "Default Mode Network" as being active when self-talk or the inner voice is present.[^raichle-2001]<sup>,</sup>[^raichle-2015] It can be viewed as the "residence" for the Ego!


### Disidentification
A thought arises, and instead of being carried off inside it, you notice it arising. In that noticing, something is present that is _not_ the thought — it is what you actually are. It can be referenced by many labels, for example presence, awareness, the witnessing consciousness, Being, Formless, etc.


### Ego
The ego is not arrogance or self-regard. It is the "Human interface"  created by the Mind for this Space-Time physical reality interactions. It is made of a "trainable and programmable brain structure" that includes the self-talk, inner voice, imagination (audio-visual), and all thoughts, including the thoughts "generated" by Body emotions (fear, stress, etc.) and feelings (happiness, love, etc.). It develops into a sense of identity assembled out of thought — your roles, possessions, opinions, grievances, your story about your past and your wanting for the future. It is a structure, not a moral failing.

Its defining need is to feel separate and to feel _more_. So it sustains itself on complaint, resistance, comparison, being right, and above all on having a problem. An ego with nothing to resist starts to dissolve, which is why it will manufacture something.

**Seeing it is the end of it**, because the ego only operates while unobserved. The moment you catch it running, the awareness doing the catching is already outside it.

>Note the fact that there are about 8,311,683,693 human form currently in 2026 on this planet, which implies there are 8,311,683,693 different Ego's with different experiences, that is considering only the Human specie. That amount of Ego plus all their interactions creates an expansive amount of noise. With such quantity and variability, any time that I mention or discuss the Ego, it is up to you to use __your critical thinking__ and decide if it applies or not to your own experiences! 


"I"
 In my posts, when "I" judges, worries, or conceptualizes, it is usually this EGO speaking — the "I" and the EGO are the same construct until they are "explicitly" seen apart.
